Your work is protected
The updater can change course content and app files, but it cannot overwrite:
workspace/ reports, models, runs, logs, attachmentsstudent_code/ your implementationworkspace/tests/test_student.py your tests
Automatic safety: files touched by the update are backed up before replacement. The updated app runs a self-check; if it fails, the course files are rolled back.
Important: a course update is not a progress backup. Keep exporting your complete project at the end of every class.
